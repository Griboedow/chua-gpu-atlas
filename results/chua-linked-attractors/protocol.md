# Joint topology of four coexisting Chua cycles

This protocol precedes the joint-link classification. The first target consists of the four already recorded cycles F1a, F1b, F2a and F2b at the exact parameters alpha = 453/50, beta = 71729/5000, m0 = -8/7 + 3/500, m1 = -5/7, gamma = 0. The four-cycle interval certificates concern this same rational point. Historical source records remain unchanged.

## Question and finite scope

Do any of the four three-component sublinks have nontrivial collective linking while every component and every two-component sublink is unknotted/unlinked? A Borromean link would be one instance; a nontrivial Brunnian link is the more general target. Four negative triples do not establish nonexistence elsewhere in Chua's parameter space.

## Numerical reconstruction

Refine each periodic root at fixed exact parameters, rather than fixing its multiplier and inadvertently moving beta. Use at least two precision/resolution levels, starting at 60/80 digits and 96/192 samples per affine section-to-section arc. Check closure, positive flight times, sampled region membership, stability, source digests and agreement with the old roots. Reconstruct all curves in one coordinate system. Any scaling, rotation or translation used for diagrams must act on every component together.

## Link computation and controls

Build multi-component diagrams from both self-crossings and inter-component crossings. Preserve component identity, orientation and components with no crossings. Repeat at two generic projections and both sampling levels. Test six pairs, four triples, all four components and the four individual components.

Control cases must include an unlink, a Hopf link and Borromean rings, including their proper sublinks. Reflection and orientation reversal must give the expected changes in pairwise linking numbers. A zero Gauss linking number does not establish that a pair is an unlink. Reduction to a crossing-free diagram with the correct component count is a sufficient polygonal unlink certificate. Failure of the simplifier is unresolved, not evidence of nontrivial linking. A matched polynomial alone does not identify a link.

If a triple has nontrivial joint topology after its proper sublinks pass, compare with a standard Borromean diagram or compute an appropriate higher-order invariant. Escalate numerical resolution if any projections disagree or crossing margins become unresolved. Keep discrepant results.

## Outputs and evidence status

Publish the complete finite outcome, including negative and unresolved cases, reconstruction data, controls, replayable diagrams, a static figure and an interactive 3D viewer with component visibility controls. Distinguish interval existence/stability of the cycles, numerical curve reconstruction and exact algebra or combinatorics on the extracted diagrams. A certified ambient isotopy from the true ODE orbits to their polygons requires additional bounds; sampling agreement alone is not that certificate.
