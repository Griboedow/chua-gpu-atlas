# Priority audit of the coupled optical operators

Audit: 30 October 2026 UTC. **The numerical observations remain interesting, but neither a new feedback mechanism nor a new topological class of attractor is established.** This bounded comparison found no verified earlier analysis of the exact six-state continuous-heat interpretation of Guo et al. [1]. It did find established chaotic relay feedback and coupled inertial-neuron systems, with consequential differences in their equations. Failure to retrieve an exact match is not priority clearance.

The [numerical report](coupled-operators-research.md), [source interpretation](light-operators-audit.md), and [order constraint](coupled-operators-order-audit.md) define the target. No simulations were performed in this audit.

## An exact normalization makes the comparison clearer

For the explicitly logistic-smoothed model, set $X_\pm=2x_\pm-1$, $V_\pm=2v_\pm$, $Y_\pm=2y_\pm-1$, and $a_\pm=2\delta_\pm-1$. With $P(D)=(D+1)(D^2+bD+k)$, the equations become exactly

```math
P(D)X_-=k\tanh\left(\frac{X_+-a_+}{4w}\right),
\qquad
P(D)X_+=-k\tanh\left(\frac{X_--a_-}{4w}\right).
```

This follows from $2S_w(z)-1=\tanh(z/(2w))$ and $P(D)1=k$. It is an invertible affine coordinate change, with unchanged time. Each unit has three states: a first-order relaxation and a damped second-order mechanical response. One cross-response is increasing and the other decreasing. Calling the response logistic rather than hyperbolic tangent cannot support mathematical novelty.

The ideal-relay version has the same form with the two tanh expressions replaced by signs, away from switching surfaces. The linear input-to-displacement transfer for each unit is

```math
G(s)=\frac{k}{(s+1)(s^2+bs+k)}.
```

The two static nonlinearities are separated by dynamic filters. Composing their scalar response functions does **not** reduce the six-state nonlinear system to a single three-state feedback oscillator. The product of the two linearized blocks is useful for equilibrium analysis, but it is not an exact nonlinear dimension reduction.

## Closest informative primary comparisons

**Third-order relay feedback.** The complete di Bernardo–Johansson–Vasca paper [2] was already cached and its model and final complexity discussion were reread. It studies $\dot q=Aq+Bu$, $y=Cq$, $u=-\mathrm{sgn}(y)$, with the example

```math
G_{\mathrm{DB}}(s)=
\frac{K(s^2+2\sigma\rho s+\rho^2)}
{(s^2+2\zeta\omega s+\omega^2)(s+\lambda)}.
```

The denominator is the same type of mechanical-plus-relaxation filter. Its numerator, relative degree, single-relay topology, and sliding dynamics differ from the optical pair. The paper reports a seemingly aperiodic attractor and preliminary positive-exponent evidence. This establishes substantial older feedback-system precedent, but not an exact equivalence or a completed proof for our candidate. In particular, its finite-frequency zeros and direct normal switch action cannot silently be removed. Our thermal switch changes a derivative tangent to its displacement switching surface.

**A six-state coupled-ring comparison.** Mondal et al. [5] provide six ODEs for two three-stage inverter rings, with diode current $J=\max(p_3-q_3-1,0)$:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\dot p_1&=-p_1-\alpha p_3,&\dot p_2&=-p_2-\alpha p_1,&
\dot p_3&=-\beta p_3-\alpha\beta p_2-\beta\delta J,\\
\dot q_1&=-q_1-\alpha q_3,&\dot q_2&=-q_2-\alpha q_1,&
\dot q_3&=-\gamma q_3-\alpha\gamma q_2+\gamma\delta J.
\end{aligned}
```

Their source reports positive Lyapunov evidence and experimental chaos; equations (1–14) and numerical-results text were inspected. This is a closer dimensional precedent, but the coupling is one continuous piecewise-linear diode law of a coordinate difference. Both rings also have their own internal negative loops. Our pair has two bounded cross-response laws and no individually closed optical loop. In the diode-off branch, the first ring has characteristic polynomial $(s+1)^2(s+\beta)+\alpha^3\beta$. At their displayed $\alpha=3.6,\beta=0.1$, the cubic Routh test fails: $2.1(1.2)=2.52\lt4.7656$. This differs from our Hurwitz affine branches. No exact conjugacy is asserted. Their predecessors include Hosokawa–Nishio–Ushida (2001) [6], whose publisher abstract was inspected but whose full equations remain an access gap.

**Two nonoscillatory inertial neurons.** The complete primary XML of Boui et al. [3] was recovered through Europe PMC. Equation (1) is

```math
\begin{aligned}
\ddot q+\epsilon\dot q&=-(q+\alpha r)+c_1\tanh(q+\alpha r),\\
\ddot r+\epsilon\dot r&=-(r+\beta q)+c_2\mathrm{arsinh}(r+\beta q).
\end{aligned}
```

The authors report chaos and coexistence using bifurcation plots and Lyapunov spectra. It is a concrete precedent for complex dynamics generated by coupling nonoscillatory inertial elements. However, this is a four-state model with nonlinear self-plus-cross stiffness, no thermal relaxation state, and multiple equilibria. Our smooth six-state model instead has a unique equilibrium and a bounded forcing law. These differences rule out a direct identification of the displayed full-state equations under an invertible coordinate change; they do not prove that individual attractor sets have different topology. No independent reproduction of that paper was attempted.

**Monotone gene/relay rings.** A ring diagram or negative sign product alone is not an equivalence test. When our mechanical poles are real, an exact positive-stage factorization permits the verified equilibrium-free Poincare–Bendixson exclusion in the [order audit](coupled-operators-order-audit.md). The present candidates have strongly underdamped poles, outside that argument. Thus a classical chain of positive first-order gene filters is an informative structural comparison, not an already verified source of the same chaotic mechanism. No claim that negative feedback alone produces chaos is warranted.

Other apparent matches were screened out at an earlier stage: “relay synchronization” often means a third mediating oscillator between already chaotic Lorenz/Rössler units, rather than a switching relay. Delay-based electro-optic oscillators also have explicit history dependence absent from these six ODEs. Such titles should not be counted as exact prior art without equations.

## Geometry and the strongest defensible claim

Letellier and Aguirre [4] explicitly distinguish a new equation system from a new attractor topology. Their publisher abstract was inspected; a complete topological-method audit was not performed here. Our existing projected sections and physical coordinate plots do not establish a topological distinction. In six dimensions, a three-coordinate projection can introduce crossings or conceal separation; a three-dimensional template cannot automatically be assigned to the complete invariant set.

The useful present claim is **a reproducible numerical study of a specifically identified six-state optical-feedback interpretation**, including a source regular control, event-aware relay integration, smoothing tests, long chaotic transients, and a persistent broad-width candidate coexisting with a stable cycle. The finite-width tanh/logistic law is our explicit modification, not the source's measured optical profile. The broad-width result has stronger persistence evidence than the narrow-width transients; neither observation alone proves a strange attractor.

Before a novelty claim, the most useful additions are: establish the invariant object's asymptotic status; resolve its bifurcation/escape mechanism; compare exact transfer functions and static response laws against older two-unit feedback circuits; and test geometry using full-state recurrence and an appropriate embedding rather than camera views. Publication value could come from a carefully resolved physical regime or transition even if its attractor topology is familiar.
