# Additional candidate screening

Status: 30 October 2026. This is a research queue, not a list of newly discovered chaotic systems. An abstract that discusses periodic oscillation without mentioning chaos does not establish a publication gap.

The current best numerical lead is the [four-oscillator synchronematic model](synchronematic-research.md). Its scope is narrow: the underlying single-particle physical model already has reported chaos, and the article and author code disagree on a coefficient sign. Both coefficient variants nevertheless produce independently checked collective-chaos candidates in our calculations.

A second independently checked lead is the [light-switching LCE slider](lce-slider-research.md), with positive examples beyond the paper's numerical stiffness/damping ranges. Its full equations are available and admit an exact event-map implementation. General relay and LCE chaos are already known; exact-model novelty remains unverified.

A third independently checked lead is the [six-state pair of light-mediated operators](coupled-operators-research.md). Its continuous-heat interpretation retains bounded chaotic candidates under exact-segment GPU integration and independent CPU solvers. The [100,000-unit smooth-profile follow-up](optical-lifetime-extension.md) finds eventual periodic capture for all 64 sampled narrow-width starts; 26 broader-width trajectories retain positive finite-time estimates throughout the observation. This is not a certified new attractor type or an experimental prediction.

Two later source audits add [photothermal Marangoni traps](late-candidate-screening-5.md), still missing a global heat-profile closure and fitted control tuple, and a [motor-contact phase-rotor network](dcr-research.md) with pinned author code. Five short rotor controls now complete, but two longer attempts remain budget-unresolved and a [strict-contact continuation problem](dcr-source-audit.md) gates the mass search. Its paper already reports irregularity; no new attractor is classified. For the smooth optical pair, a separate [order argument](coupled-operators-order-audit.md) excludes nonperiodic compact equilibrium-free limit sets when the mechanical poles are real. Both numerical reference candidates lie outside that parameter region.

## Other leads and exclusions

A further mathematical lead is the [five-state two-strain outbreak model](two-strain-research.md): 30 of 41 long GPU follow-ups retain positive exponents, with an independent CPU check and a stable periodic control. Very small prevalence limits finite-population interpretation, and the source already reports possibly aperiodic motion. A boundary argument excludes a literal interior homoclinic return to its strain-2-only equilibrium; this does not exclude chaos.

These are bounded screening decisions, not global impossibility claims. The two-dimensional exclusion applies only to a smooth autonomous regular branch with the stated state variables. A physical device can contain unmodeled memory, forcing, switching, delay or spatial degrees of freedom.

## Selection rule for further work

Favor an explicit published model with enough effective dynamical states, a meaningful bounded operating domain, and a reason to expect loss of regular motion. Check exact identifiers, author work, supplements, dissertations, forward citations and equivalent equations. Then separate three questions:

1. Does the model actually show persistent deterministic irregularity under independent numerical checks?
2. Does that regime survive the approximation choices needed to relate the model to a physical system?
3. Is the precise result absent from the literature checked so far?

The answers need not coincide. Our optical-feedback search supplied a useful mechanism and a negative chaos result. The synchronematic investigation supplied a stronger numerical candidate, a source inconsistency, and close prior art that narrows any novelty claim. All three are useful outcomes of the search.
